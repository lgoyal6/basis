What is not here

No symbols, no quantities, no dates, no filenames, no addresses. Counter names come from a fixed vocabulary, so an upload cannot cause arbitrary text to be recorded. These are counts by category and nothing else, because the question is how many, not whose.

Counters live in memory alongside everything else, so they reset when the process restarts. That is a real limitation of the storage decision rather than an oversight: see what happens to your data.
